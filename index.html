2. Bolt.new - Best Free AI App Builder For Fast Prototypes

150K TOKENS FREE

What is Bolt.new?

Bolt.new is StackBlitz's AI builder that runs entirely in your browser. It builds full-stack React apps instantly. It's famous for speed. Developers love it for quick prototypes.

It runs WebContainers technology, no backend needed.

How Much Free Do You Really Get?

Bolt.new gives you 150,000 free tokens per month, about 15-20 prompts. After that, you hit a hard wall. You cannot prompt again until next month unless you pay $20/month for Pro.

You also get free hosting on bolt.new domains. I used my free tokens in 2 days. The tokens reset monthly. It's free but very limited. Good for one small project per month as best free AI app builder.

Pros:
✅ Fastest builder
✅ Full code ownership
✅ Export to GitHub free
✅ No setup needed
Cons:
❌ Only ~15 prompts/month free
❌ Free tokens run out fast
❌ No backend database free

3. Lovable - Best UI, But Free Plan is Very Limited

5 PROMPTS / DAY FREE

What is Lovable?

Lovable is the most popular AI app builder for polished React apps. It creates beautiful UI automatically. It has its own backend called Lovable Cloud with database and auth.

It is loved by non-coders for its design quality.

How Much Free Do You Really Get?

Publishing is free on lovable.app subdomain. But to remove Lovable branding and add custom domain, you need $25/month Pro. I built half an app in free plan before hitting limit.
